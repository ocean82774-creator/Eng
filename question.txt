② favorable

① aggravate
② alleviate
③ intensify
④ maintain
⑤ prolong

3. The contract was canceled because several clauses were ( ) and open to misinterpretation.
① ambiguous
② precise
③ equivocal
④ unclear
⑤ indefinite

③ unjustified

⑤ unfounded

8. During the presentation, she managed to ( ) her ideas clearly and persuasively.

④ present
⑤ express

9. Many young entrepreneurs ( ) to revolutionize the technology industry.

② aim
